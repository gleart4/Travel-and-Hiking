Alpine Fleet is a leading ski transfer provider based in Geneva, connecting travellers from Geneva, Lyon, Grenoble and Chambéry airports to alpine resorts across the French and Swiss Alps. Our services are specifically designed for alpine travel, ensuring reliable access to high-altitude resorts throughout the winter season.

Alpine Fleet offers both shared and private transfers to suit different travel needs. Our shared services start from £20pp (€23 per person) and run in line with peak flight times, while private transfers provide a direct, door-to-door option. Real-time flight tracking, clear communication and confirmed pick-up times help ensure a smooth and predictable journey.

With transparent pricing, ski equipment included as standard and on-the-ground support at Geneva Airport, Alpine Fleet has built a strong reputation for dependable service. This is reflected in our Trustpilot and Google reviews ratings from more than 2,600 verified customer reviews.
